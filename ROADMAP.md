# CoachForge deferred roadmap

- Food and calorie logging
- Weight charts

- Weekly check-ins

- Enter or upload lab results

## Phase 4 — Engage

- Messaging and feedback on logs
- Reminders
- Stronger mobile polish
- Richer administrative tools

## Later
